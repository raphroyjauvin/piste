Piste

A Montréal bike-commuter app, in the prototype stage. It routes you along bike lanes as much as it can, warns you calmly before any stretch without one, and shows the hills before you reach them. Built on City of Montréal open data (bike network, Géobase, live bike-lane tiles), OpenStreetMap, NRCan 2023 LiDAR (terrain and building heights), STM and BIXI.

Open the appSee it on phone screens

Plan a route

  • Three options with a direct ↔ bike lanes slider; the route updates live as you drag the pins
  • Calm orange labels where there's no bike lane, and a list of those stretches
  • Elevation profile with the climbs shaded, plus uphill chevrons on the map (more chevrons = steeper)
  • Planned and in-construction bike routes (City live data) while browsing only

Suggest a ride

  • Pick a start, a loop or a one-way ride, a distance, and an optional stop: coffee, food, shopping, a park, a museum…
  • Three rides that stay on bike lanes, come back a different way, and stop where you asked

See the terrain

  • Soft elevation colours, LiDAR hill shading and contours every 5 m (bold every 25 m, labelled)
  • One-button 2D ↔ bird's-eye 3D, with LiDAR building heights and LiDAR terrain

Ride (simulated)

  • Camera low and behind you; the terrain swells as a climb comes up, so you feel the hill before you reach it
  • Hill card: "Climb in 260 m, +30 m over 860 m", with the next 800 m of elevation
  • Calm gap alerts, turn card, and places popping up on their side of the street
  • Optional chime and voice prompts; top-down view on one button

Run it locally

Double-click C:\bikeapp\prototypes\start-prototypes.bat, then open http://localhost:8765/. The basemap tiles (OpenFreeMap), terrain tiles outside the study area (AWS) and address search (Ville de Montréal) need an internet connection; everything else is local.
