Piste on a phone

How to use it

  • Use the mouse as your finger: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, right-drag (or Ctrl + drag) to tilt and turn the map. Pinch can't be simulated with a mouse.
  • Each phone has its own screen picker, so you can compare a route and a ride side by side. Reload restarts that phone only.
  • Phone sizes are Apple's screen sizes in points (the units web pages use). The notch/island and home-bar areas are approximate.
  • This shows layout and flow. It still runs in your desktop browser's engine, so the final check is the real iPhone: open www.raphroyjauvin.ca/piste in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen to get the full-screen app.
